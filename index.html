Childhood Healing, Told Out Loud.

4624: Healing From Here exists because so much of adult life is spent making sense of what happened before we had the words for it.

Hosted by siblings Ed Averette and L. Michelle Salvant, the show explores the lasting impact of family trauma, grief, childhood wounds, and the experiences that shape who we become.

Through honest conversations with therapists, survivors, mentors, and people who have turned their own experiences into the work of helping others, 4624 asks where healing actually begins — and what it takes to keep moving forward.

We talk openly about family dynamics, trauma recovery, grief, identity, personal transformation, and the lived experiences that often remain unspoken.

This is a space for truth, reflection, self-awareness, and growth — for anyone trying to understand where they've been without allowing the past to define where they're going.

Healing forward, without pretending the pain never happened.
