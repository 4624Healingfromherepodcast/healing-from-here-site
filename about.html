About the show

Why 4624 exists.

4624: Healing From Here is a conversation about childhood healing and resilience — the long, unglamorous work of understanding where we started so we can choose where we go.

Hosted by

Ed Averette

Co-host and producer of 4624: Healing From Here. Ed brings the show's conversations to life across audio, video, and the accompanying newsletter and blog.

L. Michelle Salvant

Co-host of 4624: Healing From Here, and Ed's sister. Michelle grounds each episode in the lived experience at the center of the show's mission.
