You're Not Alone in This

Resources & Support

Real organizations and helplines, grouped by the themes our episodes cover. This isn't a replacement for professional care — it's a starting point for finding it.

If you're in immediate danger, call 911.

If you're thinking about suicide or in a mental health crisis: call or text 988 (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), 24/7, free and confidential. 988lifeline.org

Domestic violence & family crisis

Related to Ep. 8 — The Shreveport Shooting

National Domestic Violence Hotline

Free, confidential crisis intervention, safety planning, and local referrals, available 24/7 in over 200 languages.

Call: 1-800-799-7233 Text START to 88788 thehotline.org

Unhealed trauma & relationships

Related to Ep. 9 — Unhealed Trauma Will Destroy Your Relationship

Psychology Today Therapist Directory

Searchable directory to find a licensed therapist near you, filterable by specialty, insurance, and identity.

psychologytoday.com/us/therapists

SAMHSA National Helpline

Free, confidential treatment referral and information service for individuals and families facing mental health or substance use struggles.

Call: 1-800-662-4357 samhsa.gov

Family secrets & truth-telling

Related to Ep. 10 — The Cost of Telling the Truth About Your Family

Psychology Today Therapist Directory

The same directory above also filters specifically for family therapists and specialists in family estrangement.

psychologytoday.com/us/therapists

Religious trauma & spiritual abuse

Related to Ep. 11 — Groomed For God

Reclamation Collective

Nonprofit supporting survivors of religious trauma and spiritual abuse — virtual support groups and a directory of religious-trauma-informed clinicians.

reclamationcollective.com

RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline

Free, confidential support for survivors of sexual abuse or assault, including experiences involving grooming.

Call: 1-800-656-4673 hotline.rainn.org

Mentorship & foster care

Related to Ep. 12 & 13 — Dr. Phillip Mobley Sr.

National Angels

Mentoring and volunteer programs for children, youth, and families navigating foster care, with chapters across the country.

nationalangels.org

General mental health & crisis support

Related to Ep. 14 — Are We Ever Truly Healed?

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

24/7 free, confidential support for suicidal thoughts, mental health crises, or any kind of emotional distress — for you or someone you're worried about.

Call or text: 988 988lifeline.org
